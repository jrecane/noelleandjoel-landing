Where to Stay

We intend to reserve a block of rooms at some of these hotels, along with a shuttle service to and from Anchorage — more details to come soon! Thank you for your patience while we get everything finalized.

Anchorage has plenty of local Airbnb and VRBO options at pretty reasonable rates, so that's a great option if you'd like more space or a home base for the trip.

Neighborhoods for Airbnb & VRBO

If you're looking at Airbnb or VRBO listings, our recommendation is to focus on South Anchorage and the Hillside (the hills on the east side of Anchorage) — they're quieter, more residential, and give you a bit of that "away from it all" Alaska feel while still being a reasonable drive to everything. Midtown and Downtown listings have been more hit-or-miss for us, so we'd steer you toward South Anchorage or the Hillside first.
